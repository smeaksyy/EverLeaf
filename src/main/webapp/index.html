About Everleaf

Everleaf was born from a simple idea: that managing our personal and professional documents shouldn't mean drowning in paper or losing files in disorganized digital folders.

Built as a B.Tech CSE Project using robust Java technologies, Everleaf is designed to simplify document management and encourage reduced paper use. By keeping everything in one organized digital space, you can embrace a more sustainable, paper-conscious lifestyle.
